Advanced BIM Solutions & Corporate Services

From discipline models and coordinated documentation to project planning and BIM consulting, Spatial Vision Tech supports AEC teams across the project lifecycle.

Our core capabilities

BIM services from concept through construction.

01

Architectural BIM

Develop coordinated architectural models from design information, including building elements, spaces, and model-based documentation.

02

Structural BIM

Create detailed Revit structural models from design inputs to support coordination, documentation, and project delivery.

03

MEP BIM

Model mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems to help project teams review layouts and coordinate building services.

04

BIM Coordination & Clash Detection

Federate discipline models, run clash reviews, and provide coordination findings to help teams resolve issues before construction.

05

2D Drafting & Detailing

Prepare and update CAD drawings, structural details, schedules, and project documentation from approved design information.

06

Construction Documentation

Produce model-based drawing sets, coordinated views, schedules, and shop-drawing support for clear project delivery.

07

Scan-to-BIM & As-Built Models

Turn available point-cloud or site survey information into existing-condition models for renovation and asset documentation workflows.

08

Quantity Takeoffs & BOQs

Organize model information into quantity schedules and bill-of-quantity support to assist estimation and procurement workflows.

09

4D Planning & 5D BIM

Connect model elements with schedule and cost information to support construction sequencing, planning, and project reviews.

10

BIM Content & Families

Create and organize reusable Revit families and model content with consistent parameters to support project requirements.

11

BIM Consulting & Standards

Get support with BIM workflows, model standards, information requirements, and practical project setup for your team.

12

Conceptual Modeling & Site Planning

Explore early-stage massing and site options with conceptual models to inform design discussions and planning decisions.
